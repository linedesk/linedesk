# Getting help

- **Questions, ideas and help with your own install:** open a discussion in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/linedesk/linedesk/discussions); for a bug, open an [issue](https://github.com/linedesk/linedesk/issues/new/choose). Say which version you run, how you installed it (one-command install, Docker Compose by hand, other), and what you saw (logs with keys and phone numbers removed).
- **Bugs and feature requests:** open an [issue](https://github.com/linedesk/linedesk/issues/new/choose) with the matching form.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** never in a public issue or discussion. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (GitHub private vulnerability reporting, or email [support@linedesk.ai](mailto:support@linedesk.ai) with "Security" in the subject).
- **Linedesk Cloud customers:** email [support@linedesk.ai](mailto:support@linedesk.ai) about your account, billing or calls.
- **Anything else:** [hello@linedesk.ai](mailto:hello@linedesk.ai).

Before you ask, these answer most self-hosting questions: [docs/hosting.md](docs/hosting.md) (servers and the one-command install), [docs/self-hosting.md](docs/self-hosting.md) (settings, ports, updates, backups) and `scripts/setup.sh --check` (which providers a first call still needs).

The maintainers answer issues and discussions when they can; there is no guaranteed response time for the open-source project.
